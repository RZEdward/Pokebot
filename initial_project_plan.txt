What do I actually want ?

project_hitmonlee_build_plan_final was a visual concept

The end goal, which could take >1 year, is a lifesize Hitmonlee free-roaming humanoid with a metallic body.

The intermediate goal, is a digital twin. Followed by a concrete physical build plan and parts list / assembly guide.


---------------------------
I believe Nvidia have opensource training simulations, IsaacSim, Omniverse. Perhaps there are others, perhaps we can also build our own.

Step 1 is to research and decide which of these opensource tools we will use.
Step 2 is to build the digital hitmonlee, shape/sprite and defined joints/points of motion and rotation, as well as realistic weight distribution
Step 3 is to put that hitmonlee in the simulations, decide which actions we want it to be capable of, code the reinforcement learning, and train his motion.
Step 4 will be coding a representation of what he can see and hear, and the computer vision to decide actions in respons to what he sees, and processing commands from words it hears, into actions.
Step 5 is iteration to completion of the digital twin, all senses and actions
Step 6 I guess is the CAD (or is that step 2?) to actually build the schematic and each component of his body
Step 7 is sending aluminium/3d print files to suppliers - and shipping in all parts
Step 8 is assembly
Step 9 would be iteration to align real world with digital twin and retraining

Start researching, and build a proposal for Step 1-3, outlining plans verbally, as well as this repo's architecture. First do some serious research into the frontier/latest methods and tools people are using to build digital twins for robots
